# Super MoneyBot Galaxy

## Goal

Build a playable Three.js MoneyBot browser game where players pilot a MoneyBot ship through a galactic money lane, collect smart-money assets, avoid debt meteors, and charge a savings shield before reaching the galaxy gate.

## Core Loop

1. Start a one-run arcade mission.
2. Steer across five lanes with keyboard, pointer, or touch controls.
3. Collect coins, index stars, and emergency-fund boosts for score and shield.
4. Enter the First Bank checkpoint when it appears to convert a cash-buffer lesson into shield, score, and combo momentum.
5. Avoid debt meteors and impulse-buy hazards that drain shield and lives.
6. Survive until the distance meter reaches the galaxy gate, then receive a mission grade.

## Learning Outcomes

- Emergency funds create resilience when surprises hit.
- Diversified assets compound score faster than random spending.
- High-interest debt reduces momentum.
- Consistent choices beat risky impulse moves.

## Quality Bar

- Mobile-first browser play.
- Three.js scene is the primary game surface.
- Complete start, play, win, loss, and restart loop.
- Fictional demo values only.
- No personalized investment advice.
